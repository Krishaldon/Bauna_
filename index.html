Eligibility Guidelines STRICT

Under Section 42-B of the Vertical Stature Equality Act, individuals with compact stature are eligible for financial assistance.

  • Max Height Limit: Must be strictly ≤ 162 cm (approx 5'4").
  • Shoe Requirement: Must wear shoes from Kids Section (UK 3 to UK 7).
  • Struggle Proof: Must struggle to reach top kitchen shelves or dangle feet off standard chairs.
  • Allowance: ₹3,000 Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) per month.
Note: Applicants wearing high heels, elevator shoes, or standing on toes during measurement will be permanently banned.
